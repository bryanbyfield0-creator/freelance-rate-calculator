About

The Freelance Rate Calculator is a free tool made by Bryan Byfield to help freelancers and consultants price their work with confidence. It turns an income goal into an hourly rate, day rate and project price using a simple, transparent formula (explained on the calculator page).

Results are estimates for planning only and are not financial, legal or tax advice.

Privacy

All calculations run in your browser. The numbers you enter are not sent to or stored on any server. They are added to the page address (URL) so you can bookmark or share your results; anyone you share that link with will see those numbers.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The site links to products on Gumroad. Those links include tracking tags (UTM parameters) so we can see that a visit came from this site. Gumroad's own privacy policy applies once you are on their site.

In the future this site may show ads served by Google AdSense. If so, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and this policy will be updated. You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ad Settings.
